# Pre-registration: are the BUYs the live regime gate blocks worse than chance? (H-RGL)

**Written 2026-10-10, before the instrument exists and before any outcome has been
computed.** What has been seen is counts only: from 2026-10-09 13:01 to 2026-10-10 00:01
UTC the live spot gate `regime_bearish` blocked BUYs on 4 distinct 4H bars (the Q&A
check, `signal_blocks`). No price after any of those bars has been looked at, and no
P&L for them exists.

## Why

H-RG (`2026-10-09-regime-gate-results.md`) found that the spot regime gate selects in
its primary window. BUYs blocked by it alone lost −1.37pp against −0.36pp kept, and
were worse than random entries in the same regime (−0.38pp). Its confirmation window
(2025-08-30 → 2026-08-30) had only 4 entries, so the overall verdict is INCONCLUSIVE and
the gate stays.

Since run 3 began, the same gate blocks spot BUYs live, often several per day. That is
the confirmation sample H-RG lacked: data produced after the design, and generated by
the live bot itself (live market-structure inputs, live thresholds), not by a replay. It
costs nothing, since the bot's behaviour does not change. The gate keeps blocking, and
this test only prices what it blocked.

## Data (fixed now)

- **Blocked signals:** `signal_blocks` rows with `mode='spot'`, `signal_type='BUY'`,
  `gate='regime_bearish'`, from the run-3 manifest's `started_at`
  (`2026-10-09T12:34:20Z`) up to and excluding **2026-11-08 00:00 UTC** (run 3 day 30).
  Rows are read from a pulled copy of the database, never the live file.
  `stale_cache` rows are a different gate and are not read: a cached replay never
  reaches the regime gate.
- **Bar:** each block maps to the newest 4H bar that had **closed** at the block's
  timestamp (bar open ≤ timestamp − 4h). That is the bar live scored. Several blocks on
  one bar count once.
- **Prices:** BTCUSDT 4h closed bars from Binance's public spot mirror
  (`hb_eval.fetch`), history from 2018 so EMA200, ADX and the ATR percentile are warm.
  The file's sha256 is recorded.
- **Entry and exit:** entry at that bar's close, levels from `exit_ic._signal_at` (live
  ATR formulas), through `backtest._simulate_forward` in spot mode: the live spot exit
  and costs, max hold 18 bars (72h). An entry restarted after a service restart was live
  up to 1h later than the bar close. That is accepted and not corrected.
- **Earliest scoring date: 2026-11-11 00:00 UTC** (day 30 + 72h). By then every entry
  has its full hold. A trade still OPEN is excluded and counted.

## Arms

- **`regime_live`** (the tested arm): the distinct blocked bars, de-duplicated in time
  order: no new entry while this arm's previous simulated entry is still open (as H-RG).
- **`random_bear`**: count-matched to `regime_live` (after de-duplication), drawn from
  bars in the same window whose replayed regime is TRENDING or VOLATILE with trend_dir
  BEARISH. Levels from `_signal_at`. 20 seeds, not de-duplicated (as H-RG).
- **`regime_only_live`** (descriptive): the `regime_live` bars whose replayed
  `backtest._failing_gates`, ignoring `confidence_first` (live already passed it) and
  `reentry_first` (not simulated), is exactly `["regime_counter"]`. These are the
  blocks that removing this gate alone would have opened.
- **`kept_live`** (descriptive): bars on which live opened a first spot position
  (`paper_positions`, `mode='spot'`), priced through the same simulator.

## Hypothesis

**H-RGL1: do the blocked BUYs underperform chance in the same regime?**
With `R` = mean of `random_bear` and the 90% bootstrap CI of `regime_live`'s mean
(5,000 resamples, seed 2026):

- **SUPPORTS the gate** if mean(`regime_live`) < R **and** the CI's upper bound < R.
- **CONTRADICTS the gate** if mean(`regime_live`) > R **and** the CI's lower bound > R.
- **INCONCLUSIVE** otherwise.
- **Power guard:** n(`regime_live`, resolved, de-duplicated) < 10 → INCONCLUSIVE.

The descriptive arms are reported with n, mean and CI, and they cannot change the
verdict.

## Discard

- **Replay mismatch:** a blocked bar whose replayed regime is not TRENDING/VOLATILE
  with trend_dir BEARISH means the instrument does not reproduce live. If mismatches
  exceed **10%** of distinct blocked bars, the test is DISCARDED. Below that, the
  mismatched bars stay in `regime_live`, since live really blocked them, and their
  count is reported.
- The price file fails to cover the window plus 72h → not scored until it does.

## What each verdict licenses

- **SUPPORTS:** the gate stays. H-RG's record notes live confirmation. Nothing changes
  in the code.
- **CONTRADICTS:** the gate stays for the rest of run 3. A run-4 backlog item re-tests
  it, with H-RG's design and its confirmation window extended to the scoring date. No
  live change follows from this test alone.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** nothing changes. The test may be re-scored once, with the same
  rules, on the window extended to run 3's end if run 3 runs past day 30, and that
  re-score is final.

## Instrument

`scripts/regime_live_ic.py`, committed before the instrument is run. Before 2026-11-11
it runs only with `--smoke`, which prints counts (blocks, distinct bars, replay
mismatches, gate combinations) and never a price after a block, a P&L or a verdict.

  NOT block** (`random_pool`). Everything else about the arm is unchanged.
- **Pool guard:** if that pool has fewer bars than n(`regime_live`), H-RGL1 is
  INCONCLUSIVE.

No criterion, threshold, window or arm definition changes otherwise. Expect the pool
guard or the power guard to bind: the gate blocks most bearish bars on which the engine
fires, so the comparison may well end INCONCLUSIVE at day 30, and then the one
re-score allowed above applies.
